## Why we are keeping this

The COSC sheet is the factory laboratory fingerprint of this movement. Our wrist experiment is intentionally different: it measures the assembled watch under actual wear, winding state, temperature changes and overnight positions. The interesting comparison is not whether our seven-day average reproduces +1.68 s/day exactly; it is whether the real watch remains comparably stable and whether its observed drift makes mechanical sense relative to the certified positional spread.
